# Problem Validation Notes — Instant Job Estimate

## Method
Observations and informal conversations with people directly involved in the current estimate process at Akbar & Sons Sanitary Store, Lahore.

---

## Observation 1 — Shopkeeper (current process owner)
Customers reach the shop through three channels: in person, phone call, or WhatsApp. For every request, the shopkeeper manually checks each requested item against stock and prices, calculates the total, and shares the estimate back with the customer.

**Pain points:**
- The process is manual and time-consuming — no shortcut exists for a first-pass estimate.
- Customer requirements are frequently incomplete or unclear, forcing multiple rounds of follow-up messages before an estimate can even be prepared.
- During busy hours, this manual process creates delays, so customers wait longer than they should for a simple price range.

## Observation 2 — Regular customer
Sent a list of plumbing items over WhatsApp for a recent job. Some item names and quantities weren't clear from the message, so the shop had to come back with follow-up questions before an estimate could be prepared.

**Pain point:** The waiting period between sending the request and getting a final price — while back-and-forth clarification was happening — was the most frustrating part.

## Observation 3 — Contractor / bulk buyer
Typically sends a long material list for construction-scale projects. Because each item and quantity is checked manually, large estimates take noticeably longer to prepare, especially during busy hours.

**Pain point:** Slow estimate turnaround delays the contractor's own purchasing decisions — the bottleneck isn't the contractor's list, it's the manual checking process on the shop side.

---

## Persona
**Name:** Regular / bulk-buying customer of Akbar & Sons
**Context:** Needs a plumbing or sanitary job priced out — ranges from a single bathroom fitting to a multi-item construction order — before deciding whether/how to proceed.
**Goal:** Get a fast, roughly accurate price range without multiple rounds of back-and-forth.
**Frustration:** Manual estimate prep is slow, especially during busy hours or when the request itself is vague, and there's no way to get even a rough number without waiting on the shopkeeper.

## Problem Statement
Customers and contractors currently have no way to get an instant, rough estimate for plumbing/sanitary jobs — every request depends on the shopkeeper manually checking items and prices, which is slow, doesn't scale during busy periods, and gets slower still when the customer's own request is unclear. An AI-assisted first-pass estimator — with a clear handoff to in-store staff when a request is genuinely too vague, mixed, or out of scope — can give customers a fast reference price while keeping the shopkeeper as the final authority on real pricing and stock.
